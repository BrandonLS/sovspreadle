Data and credits

SovSpreadle shows monthly averages of long-term government bond yields. Every source below is credited as its license requires. Charts are drawn from these figures; nothing is endorsed by the organizations named.

Our data file

The combined monthly file SovSpreadle uses, data.json, includes material from the Jordà-Schularick-Taylor database and is shared under the same license, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, with the credits above. SovSpreadle is free and non-commercial.

Facts and ratings

Historical notes were checked against central-bank, IMF and EU publications. Rating landmarks on the ladder refer to public actions by S&P and Moody's. If you spot an error, please report it.
