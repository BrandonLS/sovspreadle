Privacy

SovSpreadle has no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no tracking. It does not collect personal data.

What stays on your device

Your progress is saved in your own browser's local storage, so you can close the page and come back. Nothing is sent to us. It holds:

  • today's session (your guesses and score),
  • your stats: games played, streak and rating history,
  • your sound and music settings,
  • which history notes you've seen recently, and your recent unlimited bonds, so they don't repeat.

To delete it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting

Like any website, the hosting provider receives your IP address and browser details when you load the pages, to deliver them and keep the service secure. SovSpreadle does not use those records. Fonts and all game files are served from this site; no third-party servers are contacted.
